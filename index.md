# Thock Calc

A calculator that feels like typing on a vintage platinum keyboard.

## Getting started
- **Type:** tap a key. Keep holding and slide down to press deeper, up to lift. With Rapid Trigger on, rocking your finger up and down types the key again.
- **Voice math:** tap **MIC** and say the problem, e.g. "5 apples at 500 each, I pay 5,000 — change?" The formula appears on the green screen and the app calculates it.
- **Currency:** say "How much is 50 rubles in won?" Rates update up to twice a day and are kept for offline use.
- **Type instead of speaking:** long-press **MIC**.
- **Settings:** the gear icon. Tap the small RT line at the bottom to adjust Rapid Trigger.

## Free trial and Pro
- Every feature is free for 7 days after you start the free trial.
- Then keep Pro with a one-time purchase (no subscription), or stay on the free basic calculator with key sounds.
- Reinstalled or got a new iPhone? Use **Settings → Pro → Restore purchases**.
