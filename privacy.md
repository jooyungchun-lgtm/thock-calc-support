# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Thock Calc does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

**Speech.** When you tap MIC, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

**History and settings.** Your calculation history, voice history and settings are stored only on your device. You can clear the voice history in Settings. Deleting the app removes them.

**Exchange rates.** To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com. The request contains only a currency code — no personal information.

**Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

**No tracking.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

**Children.** The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

**Contact.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/jooyungchun-lgtm/thock-calc-support/issues).
